Know Before You Go

Which difficulty
is right for you?

Not sure where to start? Here's an honest breakdown of what each level actually demands — fitness, time, and experience.

● Easy
First-timer Friendly

Perfect for beginners and families. Well-marked trails, gentle elevation gain, no technical climbing. You'll be pleasantly tired — the good kind.

  • 3–5 hours of walking per day
  • Elevation up to 3,500m
  • No prior trekking experience needed
  • Duration: 3–5 days typically
  • Basic fitness is enough
Popular Treks
Valley of Flowers · Tungnath · Nag Tibba · Kheerganga
●● Moderate
Ready to Push Further

For those comfortable with longer days and higher altitude. Some steep sections and unpredictable weather. Deeply rewarding if you're prepared.

  • 5–7 hours of walking per day
  • Elevation 3,500m – 4,500m
  • Prior hiking experience helpful
  • Duration: 5–8 days typically
  • Good cardiovascular fitness required
Popular Treks
Kedarkantha · Hampta Pass · Brahmatal · Kuari Pass
●●● Hard
For the Committed

High altitude, long days, technical terrain. These treks will test everything you have. But if you're ready — nothing compares to what you'll see.

  • 6–9 hours of walking per day
  • Elevation above 4,500m
  • Prior high-altitude experience mandatory
  • Duration: 7–12 days typically
  • High endurance and mental toughness needed
Popular Treks
Roopkund · Pin Bhaba · Goecha La · Sandakphu

    Your Safety First

    How we keep you safe
    on every trail

    Trekking involves real risk. Here's exactly what TrailSync does to manage it — before, during, and after every trek.

    Certified Guides Only
    Every trek leader is background-verified and wilderness first-aid certified. No unverified person can lead a trek on TrailSync — ever.
    Strict Slot Limits
    Every trek has a maximum group size enforced by the system. When it's full, it closes automatically — no exceptions, no overrides.
    Pre-Trek Briefings
    48 hours before departure you receive a full briefing — gear checklist, weather forecast, acclimatisation tips, and your guide's contact details.
    Emergency Protocols
    Every guide carries a satellite communicator. Evacuation routes are pre-planned. Your emergency contact is notified if anything changes on trail.

    Each listing shows a full inclusions breakdown. Generally the price covers guide fees, accommodation (tents or guesthouses), all meals during the trek, permits, and first-aid support. Travel to base camp, personal gear, and travel insurance are typically excluded. Always check the trek page before booking.
    If you can walk briskly for 5–6 hours without stopping, you're likely fit enough. We recommend a 4–6 week prep routine: daily 45–60 minute walks, stair climbing, and cardio. Each listing includes a fitness requirement section with specific guidance for that route.
