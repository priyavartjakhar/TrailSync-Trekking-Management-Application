Platform Features

Everything you need
for the perfect trek

From discovering routes to receiving reminders before departure — SummitPath keeps every step organized.

Smart Trek Discovery
Filter by difficulty, duration, location, and slots available. Find the perfect trail that matches your skill and schedule.
Instant Booking
Book your slot in seconds. Real-time availability prevents overbooking. Cancellations are hassle-free with full history tracking.
Trek Reminders
Automated daily reminders about upcoming treks via email or webhook. Never miss a briefing or departure time again.
Activity Reports
Export your full trekking history as CSV. Admins receive monthly HTML reports on participation, popular routes, and trends.
Role-Based Access
Admin, Trek Staff, and Trekkers each get a tailored dashboard — only the information and controls they need.
Verified Staff Only
Only admin-assigned staff can manage their treks. Slot updates, participant lists, and status changes are fully controlled.

Platform Roles

Built for everyone
on the mountain

Three distinct roles, one unified platform. Whether you're managing the system, leading a group, or exploring the trails.

01
Administrator
The command center of SummitPath. Full visibility and control over every aspect of trekking operations.
  • Create and manage trek routes
  • Add and assign trek staff
  • Monitor all bookings and users
  • View statistics and monthly reports
  • Blacklist or deactivate accounts
02
Trek Staff
The backbone of every expedition. Manage your assigned treks and keep your trekkers safe and informed.
  • View assigned treks and participants
  • Update slot availability and status
  • Manage participant lists
  • Mark treks as started or completed
  • Access trekker contact details
03
Trekker
Your adventure dashboard. Find the right trail, book instantly, and track every journey you've taken.
  • Browse and filter open treks
  • Book and cancel with ease
  • View full trekking history
  • Export history as CSV
  • Receive pre-trek reminders
